Safe summary prompt 
Summarize this sanitized document safely.

Rules:
- Do not include or guess personal names, identifiers, or confidential details.
- Distinguish clearly between confirmed facts and open questions.
- If a requirement or deadline is ambiguous, flag it explicitly.

Provide:
1) 5-line overview for a busy colleague
2) Key facts (bullets only)
3) Decisions or mandatory requirements (if present)
4) Risks, dependencies, or open questions
5) What I should verify in the original document
