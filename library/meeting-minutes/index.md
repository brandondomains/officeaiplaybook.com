## When to use
Use this right after a meeting while context is fresh. It’s best when decisions and action items matter more than a word-for-word transcript.

## Prompt
Paste your sanitized notes and request structured minutes.

```text
Turn these meeting notes into structured minutes with:

Sections:
- Summary (2–4 bullets)
- Decisions (bullet list of what was agreed)
- Action Items (single owner + due date if stated; otherwise mark TBD)
- Open Questions (unresolved items or blockers)

Rules:
- Do not invent details, owners, or deadlines.
- Keep wording neutral and clear.
- If something is unclear, list it under Open Questions rather than guessing.

SANITIZED NOTES:
[PASTE HERE]
```

## Quality checks
- Confirm decision wording with the meeting lead.
- Ensure each action item has one clear owner and a realistic due date (or `TBD`).
- Remove or generalize any sensitive details before sharing broadly.
