Office AI Starter Pack — 5 Core Prompts & Guardrails 
OFFICE AI PLAYBOOK — STARTER PACK (officeaiplaybook.com)
Golden Rule: Never paste confidential data, personal identifiers, or secrets into AI tools. Use placeholders ([Client], [Project], [Date]) and verify every output.

==================================================
Rewrite this sanitized email draft to be warm-professional, calm, and clear.
