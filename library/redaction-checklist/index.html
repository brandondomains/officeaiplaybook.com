Always follow stricter internal IT or compliance policies where applicable.
Redaction + safe prompt pattern 
Before using AI, remove or replace:
- Full names, email addresses, phone numbers, and street addresses
- Client, customer, student, or patient identifiers and account numbers
- Internal IDs, API keys, passwords, tokens, and private URLs
- Non-public financial figures, salaries, or legal/HR case details
- Anything restricted by NDA, procurement rules, or privacy policy

Replace with neutral placeholders:
- [Client A], [Vendor B], [Manager], [Project X], [Date], [Amount]

Then structure your prompt like this:
Context (safe):
- [High-level purpose of the task]
Goal:
- [Exact output format needed]
Constraints:
- [Rules, tone, word limit, no invention]
Input excerpt (sanitized):
- [Minimum non-sensitive excerpt needed]
## A safer habit Instead of pasting an entire 20-page document, describe the outcome you need and paste only the minimum sanitized section required for that step.
