Four principles behind every playbook

Simple rules that keep workplace AI useful, defensible, and low-stress.

01. Sanitize before pasting Replace names, account IDs, and internal secrets with neutral placeholders like [Client] or [Project].
02. Structure before prose Ask AI for outlines, checklists, and bullet extractions first—polished paragraphs come second.
03. Ban silent invention Every prompt explicitly instructs the model to flag missing facts rather than guess or fill gaps.
04. Human sign-off always AI accelerates the first 70% of drafting; your judgement owns the final 30% before anything is sent.
